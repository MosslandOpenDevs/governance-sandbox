# Treasury Automation With Guardrails

Shift 20% of the quarterly treasury operations budget into automation experiments for delegate reporting and proposal triage.

## Guardrails
- Cap spend at a fixed pilot budget.
- Review the experiment after 45 days.
- Publish weekly adoption and support metrics.

## Success criteria
- Reduce manual reporting time for contributors.
- Give delegates clearer metrics before each vote.
- Keep community messaging transparent while the pilot runs.
